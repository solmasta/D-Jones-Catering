About Us

Family-Owned. Chef-Driven.
Passionate About Every Plate.

D. Jones Catering started with a simple idea: great food brings people together. What began as a small family kitchen has grown into a full-service catering company trusted for weddings, corporate gatherings, and life's biggest celebrations — without ever losing that personal, hometown touch.

Every menu is crafted from scratch using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients, and every event is treated like it's the only one on our calendar.
